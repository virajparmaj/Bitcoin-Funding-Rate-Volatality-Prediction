# Conclusions and permissible claims

## What is supported now

The saved RF's high R² does not establish improvement over information already present at prediction time: properly aligned current-rate persistence performs better. A previous interpretation mistakenly called that observable value the future target. The dataset contains evolving funding indications, and sampled final indications differ from official archived settlement rates. A settlement-level comparison therefore needs verified labels and explicit origins.

The current data can plausibly support the proposed lead times without a full ticker repull: all four origin records are available for 5,256 event IDs under the declared 65-minute limit. That is a feasibility result, subject to upstream aggregation assumptions and full-period label validation.

## What is still unknown

Whether ridge/RF improve settled-funding forecasts; how any gains vary with lead time or state; whether gains are stable after delays and corrections; whether uncertainty is tight enough for a bounded negative result; whether an exact prior study already answers the same question. Trading profitability is outside the minimum study.

## Conditional manuscript conclusions

**Positive outcome:** “On this BTCUSDT period and information set, model M reduced MAE by [measured bp; interval] relative to the current indication at h hours; gains [did/did not] persist across declared sensitivity analyses.” Generalize only within tested boundaries.

**Bounded negative outcome:** “For the tested models, simultaneous upper bounds on MAE improvement were below [declared threshold] at [specified horizons].” This excludes a gain of that size for these procedures, not all possible predictability or profitability.

**Mixed outcome:** “Aggregate improvements were concentrated in [origin-known state/period], with [interval] elsewhere.” Disclose whether localization was planned and avoid promoting a hindsight-selected strategy.

**Inconclusive outcome:** “Intervals include both meaningful gains and no gain.” Retain the result; seek an independently justified later sample or narrow the claim rather than expanding model search until something wins.

## Novelty claim that may survive scrutiny

An empirical characterization of residual settlement information beyond the public indication across lead times and observable states, backed by event/availability alignment and uncertainty. The scientific value depends on the magnitude, stability and precision of the measured result. No particular sign is required. Full-text prior-art verification remains necessary.

## Claims to exclude

“First funding predictor”; “new RF/GARCH method”; “97.5% accuracy”; “current indication is future-target leakage”; “null predicted funding means no exchange indication”; “all records are independent”; “no significant gain means no predictability”; “ADF proves no regime changes”; “AIC proves forecast accuracy”; “MAE improvement equals alpha”; “fees prove all strategies fail”; “leakage-free” without provenance; “untouched holdout” for the already inspected period.

## Readiness decision

Ready for implementation of a focused empirical study, not ready for submission as a completed settlement-forecasting paper. The original audit case is useful motivation but too narrow to establish the recommended contribution alone. Complete E0–E3, add E4 if state claims are retained, resolve the nearest literature overlap and reproduce all final tables. A later untouched period strengthens the evidence. Venue acceptance cannot be guaranteed by satisfying an experiment checklist.
